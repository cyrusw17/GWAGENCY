Privacy. Effective October 5, 2026.

What we collect, in plain English.

Short version: we only measure your visit if you say yes on the cookie banner. If you do, our own server counts the pages you view and the links you click, using a few first-party cookies. Nothing goes to ad networks or other third parties, and nothing is sold. If you send us a form, we use it to reply to you.

Who we are

GroundWork-Web, run by Cyrus Wilburn, Deer Park, Texas. We build websites for local service businesses. Questions about this policy: email us.

Site analytics (only with your OK)

When you first visit, a banner asks whether we can count your visit. Until you tap Allow, we record nothing and set no tracking cookie. If you tap No thanks, we only remember that answer. If you allow it, our own server records:

  • The pages you view and which of our sites they are on (the address without anything after a "?", so nothing typed into a form or link ends up in our records)
  • Links and buttons you click and the text on them, including call, text, booking and payment links
  • Whether you sent one of our forms, after it was sent (never what you typed)
  • A short list of button taps: picking Host or Grow in the price picker, moving the before-and-after slider, picking a package or tapping Book in the sample booking screen, and scrolling the price section into view. These record the name of the action only.
  • The site that sent you here, if any (domain name only), and the campaign tag on the link you arrived from, if we sent it to you (for example, "email")
  • Screen size class: phone, tablet or desktop
  • A random visitor number and a random visit number, kept in the cookies below, so we can tell a returning visitor from a new one and see how a visit moved from page to page

We never store your IP address or browser details, never use fingerprinting, and never combine this with data from other companies. Only GroundWork can see it. Analytics records are deleted after 13 months.

Cookies we use

  • gw_consent: remembers whether you tapped Allow or No thanks, so we don't ask on every page. Set either way. Lasts 6 months.
  • gw_vid: a random visitor number. Only after you tap Allow. Lasts 6 months from your last visit.
  • gw_sid: a random visit number. Only after you tap Allow. Ends after 30 minutes without activity.

All three are first-party: they belong to groundwork-web.com, so one answer covers all our pages. No other company's cookies are set on our pages.

Forms you send us

If you request an audit or start a build, we collect what you enter, typically your shop name, city, name, email, phone, and notes, and use it to reply to you and deliver what you asked for. Your entries are not saved in your browser. The campaign tag from the link you arrived on (for example, "email"), the page the form is on and, if you came straight from another site, that site's name are sent with the form so we know what brought you. This happens whatever you chose on the cookie banner, because it is part of the form you decided to send.

If you ask for a checklist or template from one of our articles, we collect your email, business name and, if you give it, your website. We email you the checklist and a few short follow-up tips about your website. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or reply 'unsubscribe', and we stop within 10 business days.

We keep lead information for as long as we are talking or working together, and delete it on request.

Payments and scheduling

Checkout is handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number. When you choose to pick an audit time, we send you to Calendly in a new tab. Nothing from Calendly loads on our pages. Stripe and Calendly have their own privacy policies, which apply when you use them.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or social-media pixels
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information
  • No cross-site tracking
  • No tracking cookies unless you tap Allow, and no third-party cookies at all

Your choices

  • Tap No thanks on the banner, or change your choice at any time. Saying no removes our visitor and visit cookies and stops all measurement.
  • Turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser and we treat it as No thanks: we record nothing and don't ask.
  • California and other US state privacy laws: we do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we honor Global Privacy Control as an opt-out.
  • Email us to see, correct, or delete anything you have sent us. We will respond within 30 days.

Email we send

If you receive a business email from us, it will say who we are, include a real mailing address, and let you opt out with one reply. Ask once and we stop.

Changes

If we change how this site handles data, for example by adding a third-party tool, we will update this page and the effective date above, and ask for your consent again where it is required.
