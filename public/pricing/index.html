Fine print

Plainly

What does a website cost elsewhere?

It varies a lot. DIY builders charge a monthly fee and cost you your weekends. Agencies often charge thousands up front, take weeks, and sometimes ask for a long contract. We are $99 today and $399 total, then $99 or $199 a month, live in about a week, because we only build sites for local service trades.

Is the booking system extra?

No. Embedding your booking tool, such as Square Appointments, Booksy, Google, or Urable, is included in the $399 build. A fully custom booking engine is the only thing quoted separately, and most shops don't need one.

How do I pay?

Secure checkout via Stripe. $99 today to start the build. $300 when you approve the preview. Host or Grow bills the same card starting the month your site goes live.

What if I don't like the site?

You get one revision on a private preview. If you don't want the site, we refund the $99. The $300 is charged only when you approve it live. After that, you can leave with 30 days notice.

Can I cancel?

Yes. 30 days written notice, no cancellation fee. After your final invoice we hand over your site files.

Will the price go up on me?

Not while you stay. Founding clients keep their rate for as long as they stay. New clients after the founding wave pay more.

Do you do ads?

Not right now. The work is the site, on-page SEO, and a Google Business Profile checklist so a shop can be found without ads. Here is how that works.
