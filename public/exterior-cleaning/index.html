Questions washing companies ask

Why not build it myself on a site builder?

You can. You'd pay a builder every month and spend your evenings writing it. What you'd still have to work out is the price list, the quote form that asks the right questions, the call bar and the local search setup. That's the part we do, and on Grow we keep changing it for you.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You own the domain. We register it in your name when you approve the site. If you leave, we hand over the site files after your final invoice.

Who updates my prices when they change?

On Grow, we do: send a text and it's changed, up to 2 to 3 updates a month. On Host, the site stays as built, and changes are quoted.

Can customers send photos for a quote?

Yes. The form asks what needs washing and their ZIP, then their phone, so you can text back for photos and a price.

I do soft washing and windows, not just pressure washing. Does that fit?

Yes. The site lists what you offer: house and roof soft washing, concrete, windows, gutters, decks and fences.

How long does it take?

About 7 days from the day we get your services, prices and photos.

How do I get more pressure washing customers from my website?

Homeowners compare two or three companies on their phone and call the one that shows the work and a starting price. Before-and-after photos, prices for your main services, the towns you cover and a quote form that asks what needs washing do most of it.
