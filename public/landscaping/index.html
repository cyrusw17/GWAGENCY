Questions lawn crews ask

What happens to the site in winter?

It stays up and keeps working. On Grow, winter is when we switch the top of the site to booking next season's weekly mowing and spring cleanups, so you start March with a list.

Why not build it myself on a site builder?

You can. You'd pay a builder every month and do it in the evenings. What you'd still have to work out is the route check, the price sheet, the quote form and the local search setup, and then remember to change it every season. That's the part we do.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You own the domain. We register it in your name when you approve the site. If you leave, we hand over the site files after your final invoice.

I'm a two-person mowing crew. Is this too much?

Host was made for that: the site, kept online and working, for $99 a month or $990 a year.

Do you do landscaping and hardscape companies too?

Yes. The site can lead with design and install work, with a gallery of your projects and a quote form that asks about the yard.

How long does it take?

About 7 days from the day we get your services, prices, towns and photos.
