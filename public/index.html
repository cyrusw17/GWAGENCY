Questions owners ask

How much does a small business website cost?

A five-page site from a small agency usually runs a few thousand dollars, and a site builder costs a monthly fee plus your evenings. Ours is $99 to start, refunded if you pass on the preview, and $399 all in at our founding price for the first 25 clients. Hosting is $99 a month. The pricing page has the details.

Do I get to see the website before I pay for it?

Yes. The $99 start pays for the build. We send you a private preview of the whole site, you ask for changes, and you only pay the rest when you approve it. If you pass, the $99 comes back. How the preview and refund work.

Will my website show up on Google?

We set up what Google reads: a title and description for each service and town, business details in schema markup, a page that loads fast on a phone, and a Google Business Profile checklist. Nobody can promise a ranking, and we won't. Our detailer's guide to "near me" searches shows the steps, and they work the same for any trade.

What kinds of businesses do you build websites for?

Local service businesses where the customer calls, texts or books: auto detailers, pressure washing and soft washing companies, lawn care and landscaping crews, commercial cleaning and janitorial companies and real estate agents.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You own the domain. We register it in your name when you approve the site. If you leave, we hand over the site files after your final invoice.
