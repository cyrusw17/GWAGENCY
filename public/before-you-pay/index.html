The four rules

  1. You see it first

    You pay $99, then you see a private preview. One revision. Nothing goes live until you say so.

  2. The $99 comes back

    If you don't want the site, we refund the $99. The other $300 is charged only when you approve it live.

  3. The monthly waits

    The $99 or $199 month starts the month you go live. We register the domain in your name then.

  4. You can leave

    30 days written notice. No cancellation fee. The files come over after the final invoice.

This job, and not the others.

This is the job

  • A site with your prices on the page
  • Your current booking tool embedded: Square, Booksy, Google, Urable, or what you already use
  • You send a logo, 10 to 20 photos, and your services
  • Live in about 7 days once we have your content

This is not

  • A custom booking engine (quoted, and most shops don't need one)
  • Ad management
  • Unlimited pages. Grow covers 2 to 3 small updates a month
  • A year-long contract. You leave with 30 days notice
