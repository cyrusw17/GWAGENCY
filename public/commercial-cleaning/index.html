    Questions cleaning company owners ask

    Do I have to show prices? Every building is different.

    No. The site can show a "from" price, a square-foot ballpark, or just "priced after a walkthrough". Most owners pick a range: it keeps the walkthrough requests coming from buildings that fit your budget.

    Will it work for janitorial, medical and post-construction?

    Yes. The site leads with whichever building types you clean, and the form asks which one, so a dental office and a contractor's turnover come to you labeled.

    Do I need real photos?

    A few help: your crew in uniform, a clean break room, a floor after a strip and wax. Until you have them, the site uses drawings that are clearly marked, never stock photos passed off as yours.

    Who owns the domain and the site?

    You own the domain. We register it in your name when you approve the site. If you leave, we hand over the site files after your final invoice.

    How long does it take?

    About 7 days from the day we get your services, prices, towns and photos.
