The site follows your walkthrough, room by room.

You win contracts on the walkthrough. The website's job is to get you there: show the buyer you're a safe pair of hands, then make booking the walkthrough the easy next step.

  1. Credentials firstInsured and bonded, background-checked staff, and certificates of insurance on request, near the top. We only list what you can show us.

A walkthrough request, not a price list.

You bid per building, so there's no menu to show. What the buyer gets instead is a two-step request that takes under a minute and asks the questions you'd ask on the phone. You get the details you need to price the walkthrough before you drive out.

Bigger buyers send an RFP instead. The site gives them a clear email to send it to and a short capabilities section to check you against.

Questions cleaning companies ask

Should my prices be on the site?

For commercial work, usually not. You bid per building, so the site sells the walkthrough instead. If you have a flat rate for small offices, we can show a starting price for that.

Can the site list my certifications?

Yes, any you hold and can show us, plus your insurance and bonding. We don't list anything you can't back up.

I'm a franchisee. Can I have my own site?

Check your franchise agreement first. Many franchisors control the website and the name, and we won't build something that breaks your agreement.

Why not build it myself on a site builder?

You can. You'd pay a builder every month and do it after hours. What you'd still have to work out is the walkthrough form, the scope sheets, the industry sections and the local search setup. That's the part we do.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You own the domain. We register it in your name when you approve the site. If you leave, we hand over the site files after your final invoice.

How long does it take?

About 7 days from the day we get your scope, area, credentials and photos.

How do I get commercial cleaning contracts?

Most contracts start with a walkthrough, and the facility manager checks your website before agreeing to one. They look for proof of insurance, background checks, the kinds of buildings you already clean and a simple way to request the walkthrough. A one-page capability statement helps with bids.
