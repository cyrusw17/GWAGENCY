## FAQ
- Do I have to sign a contract? No. Weekly mowing runs April to October and you can stop or skip a week with a text.
- What if it rains on my day? We mow the next dry day and text you when. Wet grass tears and ruts the lawn, so we wait.
- How much is mowing for my yard? From $45 a visit for a quarter-acre lot. We measure on the free estimate and write down your price before the first mow.
- When should I aerate in central Ohio? Late August through September is best for tall fescue lawns here. We book aeration and overseeding through early October.
- Are you insured? Yes, $1M general liability. We can email the certificate to you or your HOA.
