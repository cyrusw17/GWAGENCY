Questions we hear a lot

¿Hablan español?

Sí. Both of us speak Spanish, and we can walk you through every document in either language.

What's your commission?

It's negotiable and agreed in writing before any work starts. We'll show you exactly what it covers.

Do I need to sign anything to see homes?

Yes, a short written buyer agreement, as the rules now require. It says what we're paid and who pays it. Talking with us first is free.

How do property taxes work in Austin?

Texas has no state income tax, so property taxes are higher than many states. We'll estimate them for any home before you make an offer, and explain the homestead exemption.
