Questions

Do you come to the driveway?

No. Correction needs a controlled bay and an inspection lamp. Drop off in Austin. Mobile washes are a different shop.

Will one-step remove every scratch?

It clears wash marring and light swirls. Deep scratches and rock chips stay. We say so at drop-off, not at pickup.

Do you take a deposit?

$40 on a wash, $60 on one-step, $100 on two-step, applied to the total. Reschedule free with 24 hours notice.
