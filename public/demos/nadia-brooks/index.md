## FAQ
- How do you figure out what my house is worth? From recent sales of similar houses nearby, adjusted for size, condition and the street. I send a written range, and after I walk the house I'll narrow it.
- What does it cost to sell? Commission is negotiable and we agree on it in writing before anything else. Then there's title, closing costs and prorations. The net sheet on this page gives you a rough idea.
- Do I need to be pre-approved before we tour? It helps, and in neighborhoods where the updated houses go fast it's almost required. I can introduce you to local lenders.
- Can you help if I'm moving from out of state? Yes. Video tours, a drive of the commute on your behalf, and a closing you can sign remotely if the title company allows it.
- Do you work with first-time buyers? A lot of them. I'll walk you through every step and every form, and there are no dumb questions.
