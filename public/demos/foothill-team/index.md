## FAQ
- Do I need an appointment for an open house? No. Walk in any time during the hours on the board. Adding it to your route just lets us know to expect you.
- Can I see a house privately instead? Yes. Pick Buying in the form and we'll set a time that works, usually within a day or two.
- I'm already working with an agent. Can I still come? Of course. Tell us at the door, and we'll be glad to talk with your agent about the house.
- Do you host open houses for every listing? For every one of ours, unless the seller asks us not to. Most of our sellers get two weekends.
