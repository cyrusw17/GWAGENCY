Inside a Sloane listing

How I present a home, room by room. Scroll and the house walks you through itself.

  1. The great room

    Sell the view, not the furniture

    Buyers in this price range are buying the light and the mountains. We edit the room down until the windows do the talking.

  2. The kitchen

    Every surface tells the truth

    Appliance brands, stone type and the year of the remodel go on the sheet. Luxury buyers ask, and the answers should already be there.

  3. The primary suite

    Calm sells

    We photograph it at golden hour with nothing on the nightstands. It's the room buyers picture themselves waking up in.

  4. The yard

    Evenings outside are the product

    Scottsdale is lived outdoors from October to May. We show the yard at dusk, with the fire pit lit and the pool lights on.

Questions

Can you sell my home without public marketing?

Yes. Many of my sales are private: showings only to qualified buyers, and no public open houses. We'll talk through what you give up and gain either way.

What's your commission?

It's negotiable and agreed in writing before any work starts.

Do I need to sign anything to tour homes?

Yes, a short written buyer agreement, as the rules now require. It spells out what I'm paid and who pays it.

Do you help with second homes?

Often. Many of my buyers winter here. I can connect you with property managers who look after a home while you're away.
