## FAQ
- Do I need to be home? No. Leave the car unlocked or the key in the lockbox. I'll text photos when I'm done.
- What products do you use? Fragrance-free, kid-safe cleaners and a hot water extractor. Nothing that leaves a perfume smell your kids will complain about.
- Can you get out a milk smell? Usually, yes. Milk has to be extracted from the foam under the fabric, not just the top. That's the Big Reset.
- How long do car seats take to dry? Covers go back on the same day in most weather. If one isn't dry, I'll leave it with you to finish overnight and tell you how to put it back.
- Do you do regular cars too? Of course. They're usually easier.
