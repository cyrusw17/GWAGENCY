Procedure

What happens to your car, in order

  1. Inspect and measure

    Paint depth on every panel under 6500K light. Repaints and thin spots get marked before we start.

  2. Decontaminate

    Foam wash, iron remover, clay. Salt and rail dust come off chemically, not with a scrub pad.

  3. Correct

    One to three polishing stages, tested on a small section first. We stop when the swirls are gone, not before.

  4. Coat and cure

    Panel wipe, ceramic coating, then infrared lamps and a night indoors before it sees weather.

  5. Hand back

    Walk-around under the light, your report, the care sheet, and the warranty with your VIN on it.

Questions

Questions people ask at the inspection

Why do you measure the paint?

Clear coat on most cars is 35 to 50 µm. Polishing removes some of it. Measuring tells us how much we can safely take and finds repainted panels before a pad does.

Does salt air really matter?

Yes. Salt and humidity speed up oxidation and etch bird droppings into the clear coat faster. A coating gives that a sacrificial layer to work on instead of your paint.

Can I pay over time?

Any package splits into 12 equal payments, full price ÷ 12, no interest, nothing added.

I'm military and might move. Is the warranty good?

The warranty is tied to the car. If you PCS, any certified installer of the same coating can do the yearly check.

How do I wash it after?

pH-neutral soap, two buckets, no automatic car washes with brushes. The care sheet has the steps.
