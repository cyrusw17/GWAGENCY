## FAQ
- Why do you measure the paint? Clear coat on most cars is 35 to 50 µm. Polishing removes some of it. Measuring tells us how much we can safely take and finds repainted panels before a pad does.
- Does salt air really matter? Yes. Salt and humidity speed up oxidation and etch bird droppings into the clear coat faster. A coating gives that a sacrificial layer to work on instead of your paint.
- Can I pay over time? Any package splits into 12 equal payments, full price ÷ 12, no interest, nothing added.
- I'm military and might move. Is the warranty good? The warranty is tied to the car. If you PCS, any certified installer of the same coating can do the yearly check.
- How do I wash it after? pH-neutral soap, two buckets, no automatic car washes with brushes. The care sheet has the steps.
