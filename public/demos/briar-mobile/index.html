Questions

Is the total a payment?

No. Tapping add-ons only updates the visit total and the booking summary. A real site would hand that total to your booking tool. This demo does not charge a card.

Do you need a hose?

Helpful, not required. The van carries water. A working hose bib just shortens the visit.

What if I live outside the zone?

A flat $25, named before you confirm. We don’t add it after the van is already on the way.
