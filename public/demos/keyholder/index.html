Questions office managers ask

How much does office cleaning cost in Greensboro?

Most small offices land between a few hundred and a couple thousand dollars a month, depending on square feet, nights per week and restrooms. We bid after a free walkthrough and put the price in writing.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes?

Keys are logged and kept on a numbered ring, never labelled with your address. Each crew gets its own alarm code so you can see who came and went.

Are your cleaners background-checked?

Yes, every cleaner, before their first key. We're also bonded and insured, and we send a certificate of insurance before we start.

Is there a long contract?

No. Month to month with 30 days notice to stop.

Do you bring the supplies?

Yes. We bring chemicals, equipment and liners. Restroom paper and soap can be ours or yours; we restock either way.
