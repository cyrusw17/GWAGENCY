FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. Our van carries its own water and power, so we can work in driveways, office lots and most apartment complexes.

How long does a detail take?

Exterior takes about an hour, Inside + Out about three. We'll give you a window when we confirm.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave the car unlocked or the keys somewhere safe and we'll text you when it's done.

What if it rains?

We reschedule at no charge and text you the first open slot.

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay or cash after the job is done. Nothing is charged up front.
