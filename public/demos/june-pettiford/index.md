## FAQ
- Is flood insurance required? If the house is in a high-risk zone (A or V) and you have a federally backed mortgage, yes. In zone X it's optional, and often cheap enough to be worth it. I'll get you a quote either way.
- Can I buy from out of state? Yes. Video walkthroughs, a local inspector, and a closing attorney who can handle a remote signing. South Carolina closings are run by attorneys.
- What does a coastal inspection add? The usual home inspection, plus the CL-100 termite letter, a look at the pilings and the crawlspace on raised houses, and a septic inspection where there's one.
- Do you work with sellers too? Yes. I'll pull the flood paperwork together before we list, because buyers ask for it on the first showing.
