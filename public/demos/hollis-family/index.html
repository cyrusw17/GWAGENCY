Fix it or leave it?

The question every seller asks us at the kitchen table. Make your guess, then see what we'd tell you, and roughly what it costs around here.

  1. Dated kitchen cabinets

    Leave it.

    Buyers redo kitchens their own way. Paint or new hardware at most.

    Hardware: about $300

  2. A dripping faucet and a running toilet

    We'd fix it.

    Cheap to fix, and inspectors write up every one. Small items make buyers wonder about big ones.

    About $150 to $250

  3. Bold paint colors

    We'd fix it.

    A soft neutral photographs better and lets buyers picture their stuff. The best dollar you'll spend.

    About $2,000 to $4,000 for main rooms

  4. An older roof that doesn't leak

    Leave it.

    Price for it instead. Buyers will ask for a credit either way, and a new roof rarely pays back.

    Saves you $12,000 or more

  5. Overgrown front yard

    We'd fix it.

    It's the first photo and the first impression. Mulch, trim, and pots by the door.

    About $400 to $800

  6. Worn carpet over hardwood

    We'd fix it.

    Pull it up. Mountain buyers love original floors, even with a few scratches.

    About $500 to haul away
