    02 / Inspection

    Common defects on detailing sites.

    What we'd mark on the inspection sheet after looking at a detailer's website on a phone. These come up a lot.

    PanelDefectCorrection
    1. 01
      Ceramic coating says “call for pricing.”The most expensive job you sell has no starting number, so the shopper compares the shop that has one.
      Correction“From” prices for each coating level, and what changes the price.
    2. 02
      Packages don't say what's in them.“Gold” and “Platinum” mean nothing to someone who's never had a detail.
      CorrectionA checklist per package, with interior, exterior and add-ons spelled out.
    3. 03
      Paint correction is one line.One-step and multi-step correction are different jobs at different prices, and the customer can't tell.
      CorrectionA short “which one do I need?” with the swirl problem each stage fixes.
    4. 04
      The Book button goes to a Facebook message.Then the shop finds it at 11 p.m. and the customer already booked elsewhere.
      CorrectionYour real booking tool on the page, with a deposit for coating jobs if you take one.
    5. 05
      The gallery is forty photos of the same hood.Nobody scrolls it on a phone.
      CorrectionSix before-and-after pairs, picked by service.
    6. 06
      Mobile detailers don't say where they go.The customer in the next town over doesn't know to ask.
      CorrectionThe cities you drive to, right under the prices.

    05 / Questions

    Questions detailers ask.

    I already use Square, Booksy or Urable. Do I have to switch?

    No. We build your booking tool into the site, so customers pick a package and a time without leaving it.

    Why not build it myself on a site builder?

    You can. You'd pay a builder every month and do it after hours. What you'd still have to work out is the price menu by vehicle size, the booking flow and the local search setup, and then keep it current. That's the part we do.

    Who owns the domain and the site?

    You own the domain. We register it in your name when you approve the site. If you leave, we hand over the site files after your final invoice.

    Who updates my prices and packages?

    On Grow, we do: send a text and it's changed, up to 2 to 3 updates a month. On Host, the site stays as built, and changes are quoted.

    I'm mobile only. Does it still fit?

    Yes. Mobile detailers get the cities you drive to, a we-come-to-you booking flow, and a Google Business Profile set up as a service-area business.

    How long does it take?

    About 7 days from the day we get your packages, prices and photos.
