# The quote-form checklist for pressure washing

Use this to check your own quote form and page. Tick a box only when the test next to it is true on your site today, looked at on a phone.

## Part 1: what the homeowner sends you

- [ ] What needs washing, as tap boxes  
  You've got it if a homeowner can pick house, roof, driveway, deck, gutters or windows without typing.
- [ ] ZIP code or address  
  You've got it if you can tell from the request alone whether the job is inside your drive limit.
- [ ] Number of stories  
  You've got it if the form asks one, two, or three and up before it asks for contact details.
- [ ] House size or square footage, optional  
  You've got it if someone who doesn't know their square footage can still finish the form.
- [ ] A photo of the front of the house from the street  
  You've got it if the upload label asks for that shot by name.
- [ ] A photo of the driveway from the street end  
  You've got it if you can judge the length and the worst staining without driving out.
- [ ] A close photo of the worst spot  
  You've got it if you can tell mildew from rust or oil before you quote.
- [ ] Access notes  
  You've got it if there's an optional box where people mention gate codes, dogs, water spigots or tight side yards.
- [ ] HOA deadline  
  You've got it if a homeowner with an HOA letter has somewhere to tell you the date.
- [ ] Preferred days  
  You've got it if they can say "weekdays only" or "before the 15th" without picking a fixed time slot.
- [ ] A mobile number you can text  
  You've got it if the form says you'll reply by text and email is only the fallback.

## Part 2: the 10 things the page should say around the form

- [ ] What you wash and where  
  You've got it if the headline near the form names your main service and your town.
- [ ] How you price  
  You've got it if one line says what the price depends on (size, stories, surfaces), with starting prices if you post them.
- [ ] How fast you reply  
  You've got it if it states a reply time you can keep in your busiest week.
- [ ] No obligation  
  You've got it if it says nobody shows up unless they book.
- [ ] What happens after they hit send  
  You've got it if a homeowner can tell who will contact them and how.
- [ ] How to take the photos  
  You've got it if someone who has never sent a quote photo knows which three to take.
- [ ] Insured, only if you are  
  You've got it if the line is there and you can send the certificate the same day an HOA asks.
- [ ] The towns you serve  
  You've got it if someone forty minutes out can see you don't go there before filling anything in.
- [ ] Call and Text buttons  
  You've got it if both are one tap away from the form on a phone screen.
- [ ] What you do with their number  
  You've got it if one line says it's used only to reply about the quote.

Quick test: hand your phone to someone who has never seen your site and time them filling out the form. If they stop to ask you a question, that question belongs on the page.
