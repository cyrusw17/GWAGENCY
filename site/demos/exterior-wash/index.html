FAQ

Questions, answered

What's the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution, which is safe for siding, roofs and paint. Pressure washing is for hard surfaces like concrete driveways and walks.

Will the cleaner hurt my plants?

No. We wet plants before and rinse them after, and the solution is diluted to plant-safe levels.

Do I need to be home?

No. We need an outdoor water tap and the gate unlocked. We text before and after photos.

How often should I wash my house?

Once a year for most homes. Shaded sides with mildew may need it every 9 to 12 months.

How do I pay?

Card or cash after the job, once you've seen the after photos.
