Okay, now the agent part

Here is exactly what happens when you hire me, whether you're selling or buying.

If you're selling

  1. We walk your house and I tell you the three things buyers will notice first.
  2. I price it from the last 90 days of real sales near you, and show you every comp.
  3. Real photos, a floor plan and a video walkthrough before it goes live.
  4. A text every Friday: every showing, every bit of feedback, nothing hidden.
  5. I run the offers, the inspection and the appraisal until the keys change hands.
Ask what mine is worth

If you're buying

  1. A 20-minute coffee to learn what you actually need, not just beds and baths.
  2. A lender introduction if you want one, so you know your number first.
  3. Showings on your schedule, with notes from me on every house.
  4. I write offers that sellers take seriously, and I tell you when to walk away.
  5. I stay on it through inspection, appraisal and closing, and after.

Questions people text me

Do I have to sign anything to talk to you?

No. Texting, coffee and questions are free. If you want to tour homes, we sign a short written buyer agreement first, as the rules now require, and it says exactly what I'm paid.

What's your commission?

It's negotiable, and we put it in writing before any work starts. I'll walk you through what's included so you can compare me to anyone else.

Can you help if I'm moving to Tucson from out of state?

Yes. Video tours, a list of inspectors and lenders I trust, and a straight answer on monsoon season, scorpions and summer power bills.

How fast will you get back to me?

Usually within 10 minutes during the day. If I'm at a closing or my kid's game, I'll reply by that evening.
