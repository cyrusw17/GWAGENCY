The interview

“I'd rather lose a listing than lose your trust.”

We sat down with Marcus at a Waldo coffee shop to ask what makes him different. He ordered a plain coffee and answered every question.

How does a basketball coach end up selling houses?

My wife and I bought our first house in 2015 and our agent disappeared after the offer. Nobody told us anything. I thought, I could do this better, and I could explain it to people.

Coaching is teaching. So is this. Most people buy or sell a house a few times in their life. They deserve someone who walks them through it like it's practice, before it's the real game.

“A good agent tells you what you need to hear in week one, not week six.”

What do clients get wrong at the start?

Sellers want to price high and come down. In this market, the first two weeks are when the most buyers see your house. Price it right then.

Buyers think the hard part is finding a house. The hard part is the inspection and the appraisal. That's where I earn my keep.

What's a Saturday look like for you?

Open house in the morning, then I'm at my daughter's game at the Plaza courts. If you call me during it, I'll call you at halftime. I'm serious.

“If you call during my daughter's game, I'll call you back at halftime.”

Why should someone hire you over a bigger team?

Because you'll get me. Not an assistant, not a showing agent. Me, from the first walk-through to the closing table, and after.

And I'll tell you no. If a house has a foundation problem, I'm the first one saying it.

Which client are you?

Pick the one that sounds like you. You'll see how Marcus would run your game plan, and what someone like you said after.

Your first house, without the surprises

  1. A one-hour sit-down: budget, must-haves and how the whole process works, start to finish.
  2. Lender introductions if you want them, so your number is real before we tour.
  3. A short written agreement before touring that spells out what I'm paid.
  4. Notes from me on every house, the good and the bad.
  5. Inspection and appraisal handled with you, step by step, until you have keys.
Start my first-home plan

Sell one, buy the next, in the right order

  1. We price your house from the last 90 days of real sales and plan the timing of both moves.
  2. A prep list for the three fixes that matter, and permission to skip the rest.
  3. Professional photos, a floor plan and a weekly showing report.
  4. Offers negotiated so you aren't stuck owning two houses or none.
  5. One closing calendar for both deals.
Plan my move-up

A family house, handled with care

  1. A first visit with no paperwork, just a walk-through and a plan.
  2. Help with cleanouts, estate sales and repair crews if you need them.
  3. A clear choice: sell as-is, or fix a few things first, with the math for each.
  4. Updates to every sibling, if that helps keep the family on the same page.
  5. Paperwork coordinated with your attorney or the estate's executor.
Talk about mom or dad's house

Kansas City from a distance

  1. A video call on neighborhoods and commutes, so you know Kansas City from Kansas City, Kansas.
  2. Live video tours, with me pointing out what the listing photos don't show.
  3. Inspectors, lenders and title I trust, introduced before you need them.
  4. Remote signing set up wherever it's allowed.
  5. Keys handed over when you arrive, plus a list of my favorite barbecue.
